Technical demonstration · Project proposal

Enterprise AI Gateway & Usage Overseer

A governed proxy layer between employees and foundation models. It enforces role-based access, prevents data leakage and meters token consumption in real time.

Problem statement

Organisations fund premium AI access, then lose sight of how it is used.

Cost

Unmonitored expenditure

Personal and non-essential queries consume tokens with no attribution to user, role or department.

Security

Data exposure

Proprietary code, internal documents and PII reach external models. A single AKIA… key is enough.

Productivity

Productivity drain

Enterprise AI seats are used for leisure instead of core business tasks.
